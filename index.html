01 — ABOUT

Curiosity meets
meaningful impact.

My work focuses on developing reliable and privacy-aware machine learning methods for data-rich environments.

I am particularly interested in how sensitive time-series data can be analyzed, learned from and shared while preserving individual privacy. My current research themes include local and central differential privacy, federated learning, smart meter analytics and deep learning for forecasting.

This portfolio is a living overview. Research outputs, affiliations and contact details can be added as they are confirmed.
