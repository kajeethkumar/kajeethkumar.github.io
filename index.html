01 — ABOUT

Mathematics meets
meaningful impact.

I am Kajeeth Kumar G, an applied mathematics and machine learning researcher with a Ph.D. in Mathematics (Data Science) from MIT Campus, Anna University (2022–2026) and research experience at RBCCPS, IISc Bengaluru.

Holding B.Sc. and M.Sc. degrees in Mathematics alongside GATE 2024 qualification in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence (DA), my Ph.D. under Dr. S. Muthurajkumar explored optimized convolutional neural networks for predictive analytics, while my work at IISc under Dr. Pandarasamy Arjunan focused on Time-Series Foundation Models (Energy-TSPulse, Energy-TTM, and HART).

Mathematical Background: My research is grounded in optimization theory, numerical analysis, statistical learning, fuzzy temporal modeling, and time-frequency/spectral analysis for non-stationary signals and complex dynamical systems.

Metrics & service: 52 citations (h-index: 3, i10-index: 2), 4 international SCI journal papers, 5 international conference/workshop papers, 2 book chapters, and 42 journal reviews across 11 international journals plus 3 IEEE conferences.
